# Voiceover guide

**0–7s:** “Many variants, one fast order. Let buyers enter quantities across a product table and add every selected variant in one action.”

**7–14s:** “Merchants choose columns, labels, sorting, mobile layout and fallback quantity rules directly from Shopify Admin.”

**14–22s:** “On Pro, buyers can paste SKU and quantity rows from a spreadsheet, search dense variants and use advanced B2B-oriented columns.”

**22–30s:** “Large products load progressively: context-aware Liquid rows first, then bounded storefront pages up to Shopify’s high-variant limit.”

**30–36s:** “Variant Quick Order. Faster product-page ordering for wholesale, B2B and multi-variant stores.”
