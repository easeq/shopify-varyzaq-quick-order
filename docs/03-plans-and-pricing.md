# Plans and pricing

Recommended launch pricing reflects the narrower product-page scope relative to catalog-wide order-form apps while keeping meaningful B2B value in Pro.

- Free: 3 configured products, 50 variants/product, core table/cart behavior.
- Starter: $7.99/month or $79/year, 7-day trial; unlimited configured products, up to 250 variants/product, custom labels, sorting, mobile layout and fallback quantity defaults.
- Pro: $14.99/month or $149/year, 7-day trial; up to 2,048 variants/product, CSV/paste, search, barcode/case/rule/metafield columns, B2B visibility controls, per-variant fallback rules and branding removal.

Competitor context sampled September 17, 2026: Quick Order B2B Grid lists at $19.99/month; Ordexis Bulk Order by SKU CSV at $12/month; InstaOrders B2B/CSV Order Form at $6.99/month. These apps vary in scope and are not exact substitutes; catalog-wide search, saved lists and reorder history justify higher prices than this product-specific app.
